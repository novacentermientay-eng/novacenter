Placement Test

60 questions assessing 4 skills following CEFR framework. Find the perfect course for your level.

Test Structure

📝

15 Listening

Short dialogues, standard British/American pronunciation, adjustable speed.

📖

15 Reading

Short passages, multiple choice, and fill-in-the-blank questions.

✍️

15 Grammar

Sentence structure, tenses, prepositions, conjunctions, contextual vocabulary.

🗣️

15 Vocabulary

Synonyms, antonyms, collocations, phrasal verbs.

CEFR Score Scale

Score Level Recommended Course
0–15 A0–A1 (Beginner) SPARK
16–30 A2 (Elementary) SPARK (2nd half) or RISE
31–45 B1 (Intermediate) RISE
46–55 B2 (Upper-Intermediate) LEADER
56–60 C1+ (Advanced) LEADER or IELTS Prep
