S.P.E.A.K Method

5-step active English learning — speak from day one, apply in real life.

The 5 Steps

S

Study

Learn foundations: grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation through CEFR-standard materials.

P

Practice

Interactive exercises, games, pair work, and group activities to reinforce structures.

E

Experience

Real-world simulations: role-play, scenario-based learning, authentic situations.

A

Apply

Group projects, presentations, real communication tasks, practical application.

K

Keep

Maintain and improve: spaced repetition, learning community, mentor support.
