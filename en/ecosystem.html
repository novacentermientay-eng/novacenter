NovaCenter Ecosystem

Franchise opportunities and sustainable development roadmap 2026–2035.

5 Franchise Requirements

💰

Investment Capital

Minimum 500 million VND for facility setup, equipment, and 6-month initial operations.

🏢

Facility

Minimum 100 m², convenient location near residential areas or schools.

👤

Investor

Vision for education, management experience, commitment to NovaCenter standards.

📚

Teaching Staff

Recruit teachers with IELTS 6.5+ or equivalent, trained by NovaCenter.

🤝

Partnership Commitment

5-year contract, revenue sharing agreement, regular training participation.

Development Roadmap 2026–2035

2026

Launch Phase

Open main headquarters in Tinh Bien, An Giang. Build brand, core team, and S.P.E.A.K methodology.

2027

Mekong Delta Expansion

2 franchise locations in Long Xuyen and Chau Doc. 500 students, 20 teachers.

2028

Regional Growth

5 locations in An Giang, Kien Giang, Can Tho. Launch online courses.

2030

National Presence

15 locations across 10 provinces. 5,000 students/year. Online platform with 10,000 users.

2035

Education Ecosystem

30 locations nationwide. 20,000 students/year. Publish textbooks, internationally recognized Nova certification.

3 Quality Assurance Layers

🎯 Layer 1: Internal Monitoring

Teacher evaluations every 3 months. Unannounced classroom inspections. Student feedback after each course.

📊 Layer 2: Operational KPIs

Conversion rate ≥60%. Churn rate ≤10%. Student satisfaction ≥4.5/5. Output achievement rate ≥85%.

🏆 Layer 3: External Audit

Annual audits by NovaCenter team. Certification for qualified locations. Continuous improvement support.
