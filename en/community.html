For Our Community

NovaCenter doesn't just teach English — we invest in the future of the Mekong Delta community.

CSR Programs

🎓

Nova Scholarships

Full and partial scholarships for excellent students and those in difficult circumstances in An Giang. 20+ scholarships awarded annually.

🆓

Free English Classes

Free basic English classes for elderly people, farmers, and market vendors in Tinh Bien. Helping the community access new opportunities.

🌱

Nova Green

Environmental protection program: reduce plastic use, digital materials, tree planting at our campus. Each graduating student contributes 1 tree.

🤝

Disaster Relief

Donations and support for students, teachers, and community members affected by natural disasters, drought, and flooding in the Mekong Delta.
