About NovaCenter

Leading English training center in Tinh Bien, An Giang — where communication comes first.

Our History

2020

The Beginning

NovaCenter started from a small classroom in Tinh Bien, An Giang, with a mission to help local people communicate confidently in English.

2022

Growth

Expanded operations, developed the exclusive S.P.E.A.K methodology, and standardized teacher training programs.

2024

Standardization

Adopted CEFR framework, built periodic assessment systems, and established clear output commitments for each course.

2026

Expansion

520+ students, 9+ teachers, 2 campuses. Aiming to expand the franchise ecosystem across the Mekong Delta region.

🎯

Mission

Break language barriers and create equal learning opportunities for everyone in the Mekong Delta region.

🔭

Vision

Become the #1 English training center in the Mekong Delta by 2030, with a sustainable franchise ecosystem.
